# References

Every reference cited in the lab, with its DOI. Each DOI was resolved against Crossref metadata (api.crossref.org) on 22 November 2026; authors, year, title, journal, volume, issue and pages were compared with the citation. Crossref gives full page ranges for Jann (2008; 453–479) and Sinning et al. (2008; 480–492), and lists a fourth author, R. Staley, for Renkl et al. (2002), now added. For Blinder (1973), Oaxaca (1973) and DiNardo et al. (1996) Crossref returns only the first page; the end pages shown are the builder's and have no second source checked in this environment.

Checking a DOI confirms the source exists and is cited correctly. It does not confirm that the source supports the sentence that cites it; the claims in the lab paraphrase each source's main finding, and the red-team review checks them.
